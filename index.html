What we do

Grooming for every coat & whisker

From a full styled haircut to a quick nail trim, your pet gets one-on-one attention from start to finish. Pricing varies by breed, size and coat — call for a quick quote.

Full-service dog grooming

Bath, blow-dry, breed-style or custom haircut, brush-out, and finishing touches — your pup leaves looking and feeling their best.

Most popular

Cat grooming & nail clipping

Yes — we groom cats too. Gentle bathing, de-matting, tidy-ups and nail clipping from hands that know how to keep kitties calm.

Bath & brush

A refreshing wash with a thorough brush-out and dry — perfect between full grooms to keep shedding down and coats soft and clean.

Nail trims & paw care

Quick, careful nail trims to keep paws comfortable. Paw & pad treatments available verify — ask when you book.

De-shedding treatment

Reduce loose fur and dander with a deep de-shed — great for double-coated and heavy-shedding breeds, dogs and cats alike.

Ear cleaning

Gentle ear cleaning as part of a full groom to keep your pet fresh and comfortable. Teeth/dental add-on available verify.
